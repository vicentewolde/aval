Research done on 2026-11-03 to decide how to frame the submission. Official pages are linked; where a
claim comes from a single news aggregator it is marked *(unverified)*. No hackathon-winner list for Meta's
own 2026 events turned up.

## Patterns

1. Domain insiders win: a lawyer, a cardiologist, a repair technician, a lecturer. The author runs a real broker operation.
2. The problem has a person in it. The winners open with someone, not with the architecture.
3. Paperwork or evidence turned into a decision.
4. AI is the multiplier, not the whole product *(opinion piece, no named events: [Reskilll](https://blogs.reskilll.com/hackathon-ideas-that-won-2026-judges-actually-pick-examples/))*.
5. Trust and identity for agents is an active theme across events.

## Nearest commercial neighbours *(unverified, aggregator sources)*

Titl ([$2.5M seed, January 2026](https://aiproptechnews.com/article/titl-raises-2-5m-to-automate-property-title-verification-with-ai/)):
